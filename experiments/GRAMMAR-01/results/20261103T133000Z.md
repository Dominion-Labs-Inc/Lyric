## What changed

`core/semantics/grammar.py` `spell_out`: a pronoun piece marked as standing for something of its own sentence had to
find an antecedent or the whole reading was discarded — the rule that stops `Themselves are intelligent.` reading.
A possessive's owner is such a piece, so every sentence a third-person possessive opened read as nothing although
its structure was read whole at sureness 1.00. The refusal now exempts a pronoun piece that is a possessive's owner.
Reflexives still refuse. Commit `107f7664`.

## The error budget, after

Every fault of the 213 wrong sentences falls in exactly one bucket (free variables aligned first): link missed
outright 192 (33.8%), link invented outright 185 (32.6%), attachment 64 (11.3%), wrong concept 55 (9.7%), wrong link
47 (8.3%), wrong act 18 (3.2%), no reading 7 (1.2%) — 568, 100% accounted.

Weakest links by F1: `same_as` 20.0%, `isa` 42.1%, `comes_from` 37.5%, `located_in` 55.6%, `creates` 56.0%;
`member_of` 0/4 and `made_of` 0/1 never read.

Time faults, aligned: 34 sentences (6.9 points) — 13 add a time fact, 21 miss one, none builds the wrong one. The
`let` construction is 5 of them, each missing the same `follows(?v0, ?now)`.

Senses alone cost 46 sentences (9.4 points): right senses aside, wrong strict.
